# Executive Summary – Sales Analysis Dashboard

This Sales Analysis Dashboard provides an overview of the company's sales performance, revenue generation, customer spending patterns and product performance.

During the analysed period, the business recorded **1,000 total orders** and generated total revenue of **₹35,20,984**, with an average customer spending of **₹3,520.98** per order. The average order delivery time was **5.53 days**.

### Key Business Insights

1. **Occasion-wise Performance:** Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan generated the highest revenue among the occasions, indicating strong customer purchasing activity during these celebrations.

2. **Category-wise Performance:** Sweets emerged as the highest revenue-generating category, followed by Raksha Bandhan products and Plants.

3. **City-wise Performance:** The dashboard highlights the top 10 cities contributing to sales, helping identify important geographical markets.

4. **Monthly Sales Trends:** Monthly revenue analysis helps identify fluctuations in sales and recognise high-performing and low-performing months.

5. **Product Performance:** The top 5 products analysis identifies the products contributing significantly to overall sales.

6. **Weekday Analysis:** Saturday recorded the highest sales contribution, while Thursday showed comparatively lower sales activity.

### Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing occasions such as Anniversaries and Raksha Bandhan.
- Maintain sufficient inventory for top-selling categories, particularly Sweets.
- Develop promotional campaigns during low-performing periods.
- Strengthen marketing strategies in high-performing cities.
- Monitor delivery performance and explore ways to reduce the average delivery time.

### Conclusion

This dashboard helps management understand sales performance, identify business opportunities, monitor customer purchasing behaviour and make data-driven decisions to improve revenue and overall business growth.
